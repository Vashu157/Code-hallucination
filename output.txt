### Key Ground Rules of This Comparison
1. **Zero Fabricated Columns**: The base paper prints **exclusively Precision ($P$), Recall ($R$), and F1-score ($F_1$)** per dataset. It contains **no** Accuracy, **no** FPR, **no** Specificity, and **no** single aggregate "Combined" column in its main tables.
2. **Exact Published Values**: All paper reference numbers are extracted directly from the paper PDF and verified in [`paper_reference_data.py`](file:///D:/allin/projects/Code-hallucination/paper_reference_data.py). CodeHalu's recall on HalluCode is correctly reported as **$0.514$** (not 0.0%).
3. **No Circularity**: In our autonomous evaluation (`use_oracle_tests=False`), the dynamic test generator **never** accesses the benchmark's ground-truth unit tests.
4. **Transparent Dataset Status**:
   - **MBPP**: Downloaded and verified (974 tasks from Google Research in [`data/mbpp.jsonl`](file:///D:/allin/projects/Code-hallucination/data/mbpp.jsonl)).
   - **CodeHaluEval (CHE)**: Downloaded and verified (>8,000 tasks across all 8 canonical types in [`data/codehalueval/`](file:///D:/allin/projects/Code-hallucination/data/codehalueval)).
   - **HalluCode (HC)**: **Unreleased Private Author Artifact** (5,664 tasks; authors contacted via email, dataset not publicly accessible). Marked as `N/A (Unreleased)`.
